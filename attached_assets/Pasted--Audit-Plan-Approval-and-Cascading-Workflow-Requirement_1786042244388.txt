# Audit Plan Approval and Cascading Workflow Requirements

Carefully analyze the existing implementation and ensure the workflow strictly follows the business process below. Do not make assumptions that bypass any approval or feedback stage.

## Core Principle

The system is designed to:

1. Create an Audit Plan.
2. Route the Plan through multiple approval and feedback levels.
3. After final approval, cascade the Plan to Regions.
4. Regions cascade the Plan to Tax Centers.
5. Tax Centers convert approved Plan items into Cases.
6. Cases are prioritized, selected, and assigned to Audit Teams.

No stage should be skipped.

---

# Phase 1: Plan Creation

### Step 1: Audit Team Creates Plan

* Audit Team creates an Audit Plan.
* Audit Team submits the Plan to the Directorate.

**Status:** Submitted to Directorate.

---

# Phase 2: Directorate Initial Review

### Step 2: Directorate Reviews Plan

The Directorate reviews the submitted Plan.

Possible actions:

#### Reject

* Return to Audit Team with comments.
* Audit Team must amend and resubmit.

#### Approve

* Directorate approves the Plan for regional feedback collection.
* Plan is routed to all relevant Regions.

**Status:** Awaiting Regional Review.

---

# Phase 3: Regional and Tax Center Feedback Collection

### Step 3: Regional Director Reviews Plan

Each Region receives its own copy/view of the Plan.

The Regional Director must:

* Review the Plan.
* Accept participation in the feedback process.
* Distribute the Plan to all Tax Centers under that Region.

### Critical Business Rule

A Region MUST NOT submit Regional Feedback before receiving feedback from its Tax Centers.

The system must enforce this rule.

---

### Step 4: Tax Center Reviews Plan

Each Tax Center:

* Reviews the Plan.
* Accepts the request.
* Provides Tax Center Feedback.
* Submits feedback to its Region.

**Status:** Feedback Submitted to Region.

---

### Step 5: Regional Feedback Consolidation

After receiving Tax Center feedback:

The system should automatically:

* Aggregate all Tax Center feedback.
* Generate a default Regional Feedback summary.

The Regional Director must be able to:

* Review the automatically generated summary.
* Modify it if necessary.
* Add additional Regional comments.
* Submit final Regional Feedback.

### Critical Requirement

The system should automatically consolidate Tax Center feedback by default while still allowing manual override by the Regional Director.

---

# Phase 4: Directorate Amendment Cycle

### Step 6: Directorate Receives Regional Feedback

After all Regions complete feedback submission:

* Directorate reviews Regional Feedback.
* Directorate forwards the Plan and feedback to the Audit Team.

**Status:** Amendment Required.

---

### Step 7: Audit Team Amendment

Audit Team:

* Reviews all collected feedback.
* Updates the Plan.
* Submits the amended Plan back to the Directorate.

**Status:** Amended Plan Submitted.

---

# Phase 5: Final Approval

### Step 8: Directorate Review of Amended Plan

Directorate reviews the amended version.

Possible actions:

#### Reject

* Return to Audit Team for further amendment.

#### Approve

* Forward to Senior Management.

**Status:** Pending Senior Management Approval.

---

### Step 9: Senior Management Final Approval

Senior Management performs final review.

Possible actions:

#### Reject

* Return to Directorate and Audit Team with comments.

#### Approve

* Plan becomes officially approved.

**Status:** Approved.

---

# Phase 6: Distribution of Approved Plan

### Step 10: Directorate Distributes Approved Plan

After final approval:

* Directorate sends the Approved Plan to each Region.

---

### Step 11: Regions Distribute Approved Plan

Each Region:

* Receives the approved Plan.
* Sends the approved Plan to all Tax Centers under that Region.

---

# Phase 7: Plan-to-Case Cascading

### Step 12: Tax Center Accepts Approved Plan

After receiving the approved Plan:

* Tax Center accepts the Plan.
* Plan items become eligible for Case generation.

---

### Step 13: Cascade Plan into Cases

The system must:

* Convert approved Plan targets into Audit Cases.
* Maintain traceability between:

  * Plan
  * Plan Item
  * Generated Case

Each Case must retain its originating Plan reference.

---

# Phase 8: Case Prioritization and Assignment

### Step 14: Case Prioritization

Tax Center:

* Reviews generated Cases.
* Prioritizes Cases according to risk, score, or business rules.

---

### Step 15: Case Selection

Tax Center:

* Selects Cases for execution.

---

### Step 16: Assignment to Audit Teams

Selected Cases are assigned to:

* Appropriate Audit Type.
* Responsible Team Leader.

The Team Leader then:

* Reviews assigned Cases.
* Assigns Cases to Auditors or Team Members.

---

# Mandatory Workflow Rules

1. Directorate approval is required before Regional distribution.
2. Regional feedback cannot be submitted before Tax Center feedback is received.
3. Tax Centers must submit feedback first.
4. System should automatically generate Regional Feedback summaries from Tax Center feedback.
5. Regional Directors can override or edit the generated summary.
6. Audit Team amendments are mandatory after feedback collection.
7. Senior Management approval is mandatory before implementation.
8. Only approved Plans can be cascaded into Cases.
9. Cases must maintain a reference to their originating Plan.
10. Team Leaders are responsible for assigning Cases to auditors.
11. No workflow stage may be skipped unless explicitly authorized by business rules.
12. Every submission, approval, rejection, amendment, and assignment must be fully auditable and tracked in workflow history.
